World news, Wednesday 11 November 2026.

Middle East: Israeli strikes killed at least nine Palestinians across Gaza, five of them at the Nasser Medical Complex in Khan Younis (Reuters, Al Jazeera). Iran designated Argentina's navy and air force as terrorist organisations, answering Argentina's March designation of the IRGC (Reuters).

Africa: Islamic State-linked ADF rebels killed at least 22 people at eight sites in Mambasa Territory, Ituri Province, eastern DR Congo (AP). Congo's confirmed Ebola cases passed 11,000 with at least 5,387 deaths in 8 of its 26 provinces (Al Jazeera), while Kenya discharged its last Ebola patient in Nairobi and can be declared free of the disease on 23 December (DW).

Europe: more than 6,000 Italian schools were evacuated after bomb threats in a hoax wave across the continent (BBC, Reuters). Germany's cabinet approved a 24-terawatt-hour strategic gas reserve, about 10% of national storage (AFP). Millions of Spanish workers struck over housing costs (El Pais, Al Jazeera). Storm Charles shut some 8,000 Portuguese schools and cancelled day two of Lisbon's Web Summit (AP).

Americas: the AP and CNN projected Democrat Scott Colom the winner of Mississippi's Senate seat, beating incumbent Republican Cindy Hyde-Smith and becoming the state's first Black senator since 1874 (AP, CNN). Two people died and at least 10 were hurt when a three-storey apartment block partly collapsed in Brooklyn, New York (Reuters, AFP).

Asia: ten Pakistani police, two of them senior officers, were killed when insurgents ambushed their vehicles in Kohat district; the Pakistani Taliban claimed the attack (AP).

Climate: COP31 runs in Antalya, Turkey, from 9 to 20 November, with leaders addressing the summit (AFP).
